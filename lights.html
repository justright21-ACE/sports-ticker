Kasa lights setup

For The FanCave Wire. Your key stays on this computer; it isn't saved or sent anywhere except to IFTTT when you press a test button.

1. In IFTTT (ifttt.com or the IFTTT app)

  1. Connect the TP-Link Kasa service and sign in with your Kasa account.
  2. Create applet A. If: Webhooks, "Receive a web request", event name fcw_score. Then: TP-Link Kasa, "Change color", choose your bulbs, and in the color box use Add ingredient, then Value1.
  3. Create applet B. If: Webhooks, event name fcw_normal. Then: TP-Link Kasa, "Change color temperature", same bulbs, your normal setting (2700 K is warm white).
  4. Find your Webhooks key: ifttt.com/maker_webhooks, then Documentation. It's the long string after /use/ or shown as "Your key is".

2. Test from here

Test one color turns your bulbs orange, then back to normal 6 seconds later. If they turn orange, applet A works. If they turn some other color or nothing happens, the Value1 ingredient isn't being used; tell Claude what happened.

3. Type this on the TV once

Open this address in TV Bro on the TV that shows the ticker. It saves the key on the TV, removes it from the address bar, and does a test blast.

Paste your key above first.
