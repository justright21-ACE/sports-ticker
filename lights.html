Kasa lights setup

For The FanCave Wire. Two ways to reach your Kasa bulbs now that IFTTT dropped Kasa. Your key and token stay on this computer; they aren't saved, and only go to IFTTT or Voice Monkey when you press a test button.

Route A: Alexa (through Voice Monkey)

  1. Make sure your Kasa bulbs show up in the Alexa app (the Kasa skill linked to your Kasa account).
  2. Sign up at voicemonkey.io (free plan) and enable the Voice Monkey skill in the Alexa app.
  3. In the Voice Monkey console, add a Routine Trigger device for each show you want, using these exact device IDs. Skip any you don't care about; a missing one just does nothing.
  1. In the Alexa app, make a routine for each one: When = Smart Home, that trigger device. Actions = your bulbs to color 1, Wait 2 seconds, color 2, Wait 2 seconds, and so on (4 changes for a quick blast, about 10 for a touchdown), ending on your normal white.
  2. Copy your API token from the Voice Monkey console (API / Playground) and paste it below.

Route B: SmartThings + IFTTT

  1. In the SmartThings app: Add device, then TP-Link (Kasa), and sign in with your Kasa account. Your bulbs should appear.
  2. In IFTTT, connect the SmartThings service.
  3. Applet A. If: Webhooks, "Receive a web request", event fcw_score. Then: SmartThings. If it offers a color action, choose your bulbs and put Value1 in the color box. If it only offers Switch on/off, use that for a simple pulse instead.
  4. Applet B. If: Webhooks, event fcw_normal. Then: SmartThings, back to your normal setting.
  5. Find your Webhooks key at ifttt.com/maker_webhooks, then Documentation.

Test one color should turn your bulbs orange, then normal 6 seconds later.
